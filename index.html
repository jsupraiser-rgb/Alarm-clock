Age-Specific Sound Guide

0–12 months (Infants)

Prefer continuous low/deep sounds: Pink, Brown, White, Womb/Heartbeat, Soft Shush.

Volume ≤ 50 dB. Place speaker at least 7 feet from the crib. Prefer a timer instead of all-night playback.

Lullabies work well for the bedtime routine; continuous noise is better during actual sleep.

Avoid binaural beats — limited safety data under age 3.

1–3 years (Toddlers)

Pink or Brown noise often preferred, plus familiar simple lullabies.

Keep volume low. Binaural beats still not recommended.

4–12 years

Full noise palette is fine. Optional short Theta (4–6 Hz) binaural with headphones if the child is comfortable (10–20 min sessions).

Pink noise is popular for deeper sleep; White noise for strong masking of household sounds.

Teens & Adults

Delta 1–4 Hz or Theta 4–8 Hz binaural for sleep onset and deep sleep (headphones required).

White = maximum masking · Pink = deep-sleep support · Brown = warmer, less harsh.

Safety notes

Never place a phone or speaker in the crib. Use the lowest volume that still helps. Consider fading sound after the child falls asleep.

Therapeutic tones. Play pure, pulsed, or binaural-style. Can mix under a soundscape.
